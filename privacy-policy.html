Betha Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Betha collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Betha application.

Betha is a personal finance management application designed to work primarily locally and offline. You can use Betha's main features without creating an account or using cloud services.

In summary: Betha can be used completely locally. Financial data stored locally remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. Betha Cloud is an optional premium feature that requires Google authentication and enables cloud backup, synchronization across devices, and shared spaces.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data Stored Locally

When you use Betha without Betha Cloud, the information you enter into the application is stored locally on your device.

This information may include:

  • Income and expenses.
  • Accounts and balances.
  • Transfers.
  • Categories and subcategories.
  • Budgets.
  • Recurring transactions.
  • Goals, debts, and other financial information entered by you.
  • Application preferences and settings.

This information is not transmitted to our servers simply by using Betha in local mode.

1.2 Betha Cloud

Betha Cloud is an optional premium feature that allows you to back up your data to the cloud, synchronize your information across multiple devices, and use shared spaces.

If you enable Betha Cloud, certain Betha data may be stored in Google Cloud Firestore to provide these features.

Using Betha Cloud requires signing in with a Google account.

2. Google Sign-In

Betha uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users who choose to use Betha Cloud.

Google may provide basic information associated with your Google account, such as your user identifier, name, and email address, depending on the permissions granted and the applicable Google services.

This information is used to identify your Betha Cloud account, provide authentication, and enable cloud synchronization and related features.

3. Google Cloud Firestore

Betha Cloud uses Google Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize the data required for its cloud features.

Data stored in Firestore may include financial information entered by you, application settings, synchronization information, and data associated with shared spaces.

Access to this information is restricted through authentication and Firestore security rules. Your data is not intended to be accessible by other users unless you voluntarily participate in a shared space.

4. Shared Spaces

Betha allows users to create shared spaces through Betha Cloud. When you participate in a shared space, certain financial information associated with that space may be accessible to other authorized members of the same space.

By using a shared space, you acknowledge that information added to that space may be visible to its authorized members.

5. Data and Account Deletion

Betha allows you to delete your data stored in Betha Cloud and delete your Betha Cloud account.

When you delete your account or associated cloud data, the applicable data is deleted according to the deletion processes implemented by Betha and the cloud infrastructure services used by the application.

Data stored exclusively on your device can be deleted through the application or by deleting Betha's data from your device.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

Betha uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, diagnose, and fix application crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may collect technical information related to application crashes, such as device information, application version, operating system information, technical logs, and information related to the application state at the time of a crash.

Crashlytics is used to improve the stability, reliability, and technical performance of Betha.

7. Biometric Authentication

Betha may use biometric authentication available on your device, such as fingerprint or facial recognition, to help protect access to the application.

Betha does not receive or store your biometric data. Biometric authentication is handled by the operating system and the secure hardware or security mechanisms provided by your device.

8. Exchange Rates

Betha uses Frankfurter to obtain currency exchange rates.

When the application requests updated exchange-rate information, it may communicate with services provided by Frankfurter. Exchange-rate information may be stored locally so that it can be used later, including when the device is offline.

9. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and maintain Betha's features, including:

  • Managing income, expenses, and accounts.
  • Creating and monitoring budgets.
  • Providing cloud backups through Betha Cloud.
  • Synchronizing information across devices.
  • Providing shared spaces.
  • Authenticating Betha Cloud users.
  • Detecting and resolving technical problems.
  • Obtaining currency exchange rates.
  • Improving the stability and functionality of the application.

10. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to provide certain Betha features, including Google for authentication and cloud services, Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, and Frankfurter for currency exchange rates.

These third-party services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect information used by Betha Cloud.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Offline Use

One of Betha's main features is the ability to use the application locally and without an Internet connection.

Betha Cloud is not required to use Betha's core personal finance management features.

13. Third-Party Services

Betha uses third-party services for certain features. These services have their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Google Sign-In and Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Frankfurter.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers to understand how they handle information processed through their services.

14. Children's Privacy

Betha is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes other than those necessary to provide the application's features.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to Betha, its features, or the services we use.

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy.
